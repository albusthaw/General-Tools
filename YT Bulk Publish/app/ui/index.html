How this works

  1. Pick the window where you are signed in to YouTube Studio.
  2. If that browser was opened normally, the tool cannot reach inside it yet. You can then let the tool open its own browser window. You sign in there once, and the sign-in is remembered for next time.
  3. After that, the tool reads your video list and makes the changes you choose, one video at a time, right in the browser so you can watch it work.
